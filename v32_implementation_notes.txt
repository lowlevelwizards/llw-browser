Low Level Wizards HTML Starter v32 — staged implementation notes

Implemented:
- Larger stumps and boulders, plus more boulders overall.
- Stones now have more size/color/shape variation.
- Fallen logs can spawn with a small side branch.
- Decorative groundcover pass:
  - leaf litter
  - clover patches
  - moss patches
  - small wildflower patches
  - pebble patches
  - rounded grass tufts
- Placement now respects visible water footprint better:
  - trees/bushes/mushrooms/props should avoid spawning in visually watery footing.
- Stronger terrain hue shifting:
  - lighter terrain reads warmer
  - darker/shaded terrain reads cooler
  - wooded/shaded floor differences are more dramatic.
- Bushes and mushroom piles sit slightly lower visually.

Key changed files:
- game-data.js
- ecology.js
- landscape-render.js
- render.js
