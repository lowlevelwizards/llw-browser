Low Level Wizards Browser — v59 The Dark Between Fires v0

MISSION
Make night a real spatial condition and turn the existing cloth + stick + campfire relationship into a portable-light preparation loop.

CORE STORY
ruin basket -> cloth scrap -> bring home -> combine with stick -> light at campfire -> sacrifice held hand -> travel after dark

IMPLEMENTED

1. Explicit Use-action seam
- use-actions.js captures the current legacy interaction chain as one provider.
- New providers can register with explicit priorities instead of wrapping getUseAction again.
- Legacy priority preserves important existing behavior:
  guild settlement > torch work > fire work > casual Guild talk > storage > forage > eating.
- Torch actions use priority 90.

2. Shared lighting truth
- lighting.js owns light-source providers.
- Campfire is the first built-in source.
- Sources expose world position, full radius, falloff radius, intensity and visual glow data.
- LLW.lighting.sampleAt(x, y) and visual glow rendering use the same source definitions.

3. Physical torches
- New Torch item, pocketable=false.
- New Spent Torch item, pocketable=false.
- Beside the firepit, holding a Stick while carrying a Cloth Scrap offers Wrap Torch.
- The exact stick and cloth instances are consumed and replaced by one physical Torch item in hand.
- Wrapping costs one turn.
- Holding an unlit Torch beside a lit campfire offers Light Torch.
- Lighting costs one turn.
- Torch state lives directly on the saved item:
  state.lit
  state.fuelTurns
  state.maxFuelTurns
- Lit state and remaining fuel therefore persist automatically through the existing item snapshot.

4. Torch fuel
- Maximum fuel: 60 turns (~3 in-world hours at 20 turns/hour).
- Rain increases fuel consumption continuously up to 1.40x at full rain intensity.
- No random extinguishing.
- On burnout the physical item becomes a Spent Torch instead of disappearing.
- Lit torches remain light sources wherever the item physically exists: held, dropped, camp basket, or ruin basket.

5. Darkness / visibility
- visibility.js exposes LLW.visibility.getVisibilityAt(worldX, worldY).
- Day remains fully visible.
- Twilight/night progressively reduce useful visual range around the player.
- Clear deep-night local awareness is about 4.2 tiles.
- Cloud and rain reduce that radius; the minimum is about 2.35 tiles.
- Deep darkness retains a low silhouette floor rather than deleting the world.
- Campfire and torch light raise visibility through LLW.lighting.
- Familiar player-made trails receive a subtle readability bonus after repeated use.
- Overview/debug world view deliberately bypasses the darkness mask.

6. Presentation
- Darkness is a screen-space overlay with soft revealed regions around the wizard and real light sources.
- Mechanical light radii are stable; visual glows may flicker slightly.
- Torch appearance is registered through item-views.js rather than special-cased into render.js.
- Torch can render held, dropped, or peeking from physical storage.

TUNING
visibility.js:
- clearNightRadius 4.2
- minimumNightRadius 2.35
- cloudRadiusPenalty 0.80
- rainRadiusPenalty 0.55
- silhouetteFloor 0.18
- trailBonusMax 0.12
- trailBonusStartUse 12
- trailBonusFullUse 36

torch.js:
- MAX_FUEL_TURNS 60
- RAIN_BURN_MULTIPLIER 1.40
- LIGHT_RADIUS 3.8
- FULL_LIGHT_RADIUS 1.35

EXPLICITLY OUT OF SCOPE
- enemies / stealth
- fear or darkness damage
- fog-of-war / explored map
- lanterns / lamp oil
- general crafting menus or recipe systems
- wet clothing / tinder / ignition failure
- throwing torches
- fire propagation
- rebuilding ruins

ACCEPTANCE STORY
I find cloth in an old ruin and bring it home. Beside the campfire I use a stick and the cloth to wrap a torch, light it, and leave after dark. The torch occupies my hand and lets me read the nearby country. Rain shortens how long it lasts. On the way back I can make out an established route more easily, and the campfire becomes a real beacon through the dark.
