Low Level Wizards Browser — v56 Old Places / Procedural Ruins v0

PURPOSE
Give the generated woodland persistent destinations that existed before the wizard arrived. This build proves place-generation and stable spatial identity before loot containers, cloth scraps, darkness, or torches.

CORE RULE
Landmarks are seed truth, not save payload. The seed answers “what was here.” Future mutable contents answer “what happened here.”

IMPLEMENTED

1. Wayside ruin family
- One deterministic procedural landmark family: mossy old wayside ruins.
- Four authored macro variants: broken corner, three-wall shelter, broken foundation, crooked remnant.
- Macro shapes stay readable; rotation, mossiness, site, and wall variation differ by seed.

2. Sparse placement
- One primary ruin is sought far enough from camp to require an outing.
- ~36% chance to seek a second ruin.
- Ruins keep distance from camp and from one another.
- Placement scores existing baseline terrain: flatness, dryness, openness, clearing influence, surrounding woodland.
- Standing water is forbidden.
- Major existing trees/boulders/logs are avoided; the relaxed fallback still refuses heavily obstructed sites.
- Weather does not affect ruin generation.

3. Stable spatial identity / future sockets
Each ruin has a stable seed-derived id and exposes:
- entrance anchor
- container anchor
- light anchor
- several interior anchors

Public API:
- LLW.landmarks.getAll()
- LLW.landmarks.getById(id)
- LLW.landmarks.getAnchor(landmarkId, kind)
- LLW.landmarks.getBlockingFootprints()
- LLW.landmarks.getBlockingCellKeys()

The container/light anchors are intentionally empty in v56. They are staging points for later baskets/cloth and torches/sconces rather than content spawned prematurely.

4. Physical walls
- Surviving stone-wall segments are true blockers.
- Movement can pass through actual gaps/openings but not through visible intact wall segments.
- Collision uses low capsule footprints around each wall, not a giant invisible square around the whole ruin.

5. World-generation relationships
- Landmarks generate immediately before natural crossings are resolved.
- During crossing generation, ruin wall cells are temporarily exposed as blockers so bridges/stepping-stone placement does not run through masonry.
- During historical trail generation, ruin walls are likewise blockers.
- ~72% of worlds with a ruin offer the primary ruin as an additional old-path destination candidate. The existing trail generator still decides whether that ruin is actually the selected clearing destination.
- Ordinary generated sticks/mushrooms that would land in the reserved ruin footprint are displaced to nearby safe ground.

6. Presentation
- Old floor remnants render low in the scene so vegetation/props can interrupt them.
- Mossy low walls render with chipped-block segmentation and warm/cool stone variation.
- Walls are rendered once with the ground and then only wall segments physically in front of the wizard are re-drawn above the scene. This gives practical front/back occlusion without replacing the renderer’s existing depth queue.
- Existing groundcover remains free to grow through the ruin, helping it read as old rather than freshly stamped onto the map.

PERSISTENCE
- No landmark geometry is serialized by v54.
- Reload regenerates the same ruins from the same seed before lived history is applied.
- A new seed generates different landmark placement/variant/rotation.
- Future basket/container state should persist through physical item/container history rather than a generic “ruin looted” flag.

VALIDATION
- landmarks.js passes Node syntax checking.
- Focused harness verifies:
  - at least one landmark in a viable world
  - same seed reproduces identical landmark geometry/anchors
  - different seed changes landmark result
  - entrance/container/light anchors exist
  - crossing generation sees temporary ruin blockers
  - trail generation sees temporary ruin blockers
  - temporary blockers do not leak into the real boulder list
  - cardinal player movement is blocked by ruin walls
  - ordinary forage is displaced out of reserved ruin space
  - landmark may be exposed as a historical-trail destination candidate

ACCEPTANCE PLAYTEST
1. Generate/continue a world and locate the ruin naturally.
2. Confirm it is well away from camp and reads as a destination rather than camp clutter.
3. Walk completely around it.
4. Enter through a genuine opening.
5. Attempt to walk through intact visible wall segments; movement must stop.
6. Verify vegetation/groundcover partially interrupts and overgrows the ruin.
7. Check whether an old trail sometimes appears to lead toward the place, without requiring one every seed.
8. Reload the same world; the ruin must return in exactly the same place/form.
9. Generate a different seed; placement/orientation/variant should differ.

SUCCESS STORY
“I followed an old path away from camp and came into a clearing with broken mossy walls. I could circle the place, slip through a collapsed side, and stand inside it. There was nothing to loot yet, but it clearly had places where something could have been left. When I came back later, it was still the same ruin.”
