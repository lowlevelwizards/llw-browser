Low Level Wizards — v44 Papercraft Sun Shadows

Purpose
Make the time-of-day shadow experiment unmistakable at phone scale, while keeping the renderer painterly and cheap.

Changes
- Retuned the 2D art-direction sun model:
  - long morning/evening shadows
  - compact noon shadows
  - stronger cool blue-green cast-shadow value
- Trees now cast a layered papercraft canopy silhouette rather than a single timid fuzzy ellipse.
- Tree shadow lobes preserve the crown's chunky layered character.
- Bush, boulder, stump, fallen-log, bramble, bridge, and stepping-stone cast shadows were strengthened proportionally.
- Shadows are rendered above water/terrain/ambient groundcover but below physical props, preserving the rule that shadows darken the ground rather than painting over objects.
- Grass now responds to both persistent ecological shade/canopy and current moving sun shadow.
- The player still receives current cast shade when walking through it.
- Added explicit cache-busting in index.html:
  - time.js?v=44
  - render.js?v=44

Expected visual test
- Around 08:00: tree shadows should clearly lean left/west and extend roughly around a tile or more.
- Around noon: shadows should pull close beneath trees.
- Around 16:00–18:00: shadows should clearly swing right/east and lengthen again.
- Grass under woodland canopy should no longer remain bright yellow-green in deep tree shade.

Files changed

Validation
