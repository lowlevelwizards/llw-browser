v59.1 — Hand + Ground Crafting / Interaction Intent

Purpose
-------
Loosen the v59 torch recipe so it follows physical possessions instead of the campfire, while preserving an intuitive way to feed the fire when Stick + Cloth are both available.

Interaction grammar
-------------------
- Wrapping a torch does NOT require the firepit.
- Recipe ingredients may come from the player's carried items (held/pockets) or world items on the player's current tile.
- If the hand is empty, a valid Stick + Cloth recipe can be assembled from pockets/ground and the resulting Torch is placed in hand.
- If one recipe ingredient is held, the other may be in a pocket or on the ground at the player's feet.
- If a non-recipe item occupies the hand, ground/pocket crafting is not offered; the player must free the hand.
- Beside a fire that can accept fuel, a held Stick expresses "feed the fire" intent. Add Stick therefore beats Wrap Torch even if Cloth is carried or on the ground.
- Holding Cloth beside the same fire expresses crafting intent, so Cloth + accessible Stick yields Wrap Torch.
- Away from a usable fire, held Stick + accessible Cloth yields Wrap Torch normally.

Lighting
--------
Lighting an already-wrapped Torch still requires a lit campfire in v59.1. This pass changes assembly, not ignition.

Scope exclusions
----------------
- no general recipe/crafting framework
- no crafting UI/menu
- no adjacent-tile ingredient vacuuming
- no automatic crafting while holding an unrelated item
- no alternate-action carousel yet

Acceptance checks
-----------------
1. Held Stick + carried Cloth beside fire => Add Stick remains available.
2. Held Cloth + carried/ground Stick beside fire => Wrap Torch.
3. Held Stick + carried/ground Cloth away from fire => Wrap Torch.
4. Empty hand + Stick + Cloth on current ground tile => Wrap Torch into hand.
5. Empty hand + pocket/ground mix => Wrap Torch into hand.
6. Ingredient instances are consumed physically; one Torch instance is created.
