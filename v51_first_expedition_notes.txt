Low Level Wizards browser — v51 First Expedition / Guild Slip v0

Purpose
-------
Cross the project from "world worth wandering" into "world gives me a reason to wander."

This pass deliberately does not add a generic quest framework, rewards economy,
waypoints, quest-item tags, combat, or procedural dialogue.

What changed
------------
- Added Marn, one physical requester beside the camp fire.
- Marn chooses an actually clear nearby camp tile from generated world truth.
- Marn blocks movement like a person rather than behaving as decoration.
- Added one Guild Slip: Fungal Procurement.
- Interact with Marn using the existing Use/F action to take the slip.
- The destination hint is derived from ordinary mushroom locations and their
  existing moisture/shade ecology.
- No waypoint is shown. The slip gives rough distance, direction, and habitat.
- The objective asks for 3 ordinary mushrooms. They are not tagged or spawned
  specially for the task.
- Returning to Marn offers delivery through the existing Use/F action.
- Delivery consumes the real mushroom instances in hand/pockets.
- Full and partial settlement are supported.
- Settlement records an archived slip outcome.
- Returning at night records AFTER DARK.
- A full delivery changes Marn from stranger -> knows_your_name.
- A partial delivery changes Marn from stranger -> knows_your_face.
- Added a small paper Guild Slip HUD rather than a conventional quest tracker.

Architecture
------------
Content defines the slip and requester.
State records current/archived Guild truth.
guild.js owns acceptance, ecology-derived guidance, settlement, and relationship.
systems.js only routes movement/Use into that truth.
main.js only presents the current slip.
render.js only presents Marn.

World truth remains separate from player knowledge:
the destination stores a real world anchor, while the player only sees a rough
direction/habitat description.

Validation
----------
- All changed JavaScript parses successfully.
- Mock expedition verified:
  take slip -> derived mushroom cluster -> carry 3 ordinary mushrooms ->
  return -> physical mushrooms removed -> outcome archived ->
  AFTER DARK recorded -> Marn knows your name.
- Marn placement test verified that a blocked preferred camp tile causes Marn
  to choose another clear nearby tile.

Acceptance test
---------------
A run should now be able to become:
"Marn sent me out for mushrooms. I followed their rough directions into damp
shade, got held up by the landscape, and came back with what I could carry."

That sentence should be produced by existing ecology, traversal, inventory,
time, and fire systems rather than scripted quest staging.
