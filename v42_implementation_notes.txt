LOW LEVEL WIZARDS — v42 Visual / Grounding Pass
Painted Water & Lived-In Grass

Built on v41 Water, Foliage & Thicket Language.

CHANGED

landscape-render.js
- Rebuilt water presentation around a clearer material grammar:
  damp/silty bank -> shallow shelf -> main water body -> pooled/deeper wash -> sparse surface marks.
- Main water union is now an opaque painted base instead of relying on overlapping translucent feature fills.
- Removed generic shallow-edge strokes from channel polygons. This specifically prevents pale rectangular/ghost seams where channel geometry overlaps ponds.
- Standing water retains a restrained inner shallow shelf because pond/lake edges are plausibly shallower.
- Added local per-water-body painted wash/depth treatment rather than one giant screen-space gradient shared by unrelated ponds.
- Added a restrained pale center band to exposed creek/channel runs to imply shallower, constrained moving water.
- Channel center highlights are clipped to the actual channel ribbon so they cannot bleed onto land.
- Added semantic mouth treatment where channel centerlines cross into/out of standing water: a pale shallow fan appears inside the pond at the real connection.
- Stepping-stone crossings now create a localized shallow-water cue beneath the crossing.
- Reworked pond ripples into one or two loose, slightly angled curved glints with subtle breathing movement instead of the conventional repeated straight water-line icon.
- Preserved the improved rounded weak-channel terminal / seep / ditch behavior.

render.js
- Grass tufts are wider and more substantial at phone scale.
- Individual blades are taller, wider and chunkier while keeping flat bases and rounded tips.
- Added a foreground-groundcover render path. Grass marked as foreground is Y-sorted with trees, boulders, logs, stumps and the player so it can overlap the visible base of props and visually plant them into the ground.

 ecology.js
- Grass no longer spawns mainly as generic open-ground scatter.
- Ambient grass now explicitly favors relationships:
  * meadow/open ground
  * water margins
  * path shoulders rather than path centers
  * mud rims rather than muddy cores
  * rings around disturbed/cleared ground
  * sheltered ground around existing props
- Added an independent deterministic prop-grounding grass pass.
- Grass commonly gathers in front of / around the bases of trees, boulders, stumps and fallen logs.
- Fallen-log bridges are excluded from prop grass so grass is not planted over water crossings.
- The prop-grass pass has its own RNG namespace so it remains deterministic without perturbing the existing ambient groundcover stream.

game-data.js
- Grass tuft defaults were enlarged slightly to support the new chunkier visual language.
- addGrassTuft now supports deterministic exact placement options for relationship-driven / foreground tufts without consuming the ordinary initial-entity RNG stream.

INTENTION
Water should read as a painted depression with understandable depth cues, not a blue polygon plus decorative highlights:
- bank stain means wet earth
- shelf means shallow shoreline
- pale creek center means constrained/shallow moving water
- mouth fan means an actual stream/pond join
- localized crossing shelf means shallow usable footing
- darker pooled wash means deeper/still water

Grass should make objects and terrain relationships feel inhabited:
- grass grows around things
- gathers at edges
- survives beside paths
- circles worn/muddy ground
- catches at the feet of old wood and stone
rather than appearing as uniformly scattered filler.
